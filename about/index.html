About

How I work

From the workflow to the working product.

01

Map the work

Start with the people, steps, and decisions behind a workflow. Find out what someone needs at each handoff.

02

Prototype the flow

Turn the workflow into wireframes and clickable Figma prototypes so the team can review the important interactions early.

03

Build reusable patterns

Use components and clear states to keep related screens consistent across modules and breakpoints.

04

Verify in the build

Work with developers at handoff and compare the implementation with approved designs during UAT.
