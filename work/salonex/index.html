Designing for the salon front desk.

A salon platform spanning booking, client management, and checkout. My work covered services, accounts, and point-of-sale interfaces.

Services, accounts, point of sale

01

Context

What does a front desk team need to see when a visit turns into a sale?

Salonex brings appointment and client information into the same product as service management and point of sale. The design challenge is deciding which details belong in each view, especially when staff switch tasks during a visit.

Explore the workflow

Three views into the design work.

01 / SERVICES

Start with the service

I contributed to service interfaces within the salon operations product. The service is the common reference across booking and checkout, so its details need to be easy to identify.

Design lens · Consistent product language
02 / POINT OF SALE

Make payment information legible

I worked on point-of-sale interfaces. At checkout, staff need to see the service being charged and the payment information together so they can review the transaction.

Design lens · Transaction clarity
03 / ACCOUNTS

Separate setup from the transaction

I contributed to account management interfaces alongside POS work. Account management and checkout serve different tasks, even when both use the same payment information.

Design lens · Task boundaries
