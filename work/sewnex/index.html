TAILORING ERP

From order intake to the workshop.

An ERP for tailoring businesses. I redesigned the order view around manufacturing activities, scheduled dates, and the instructions teams need to carry an order through production.

Role
Order view redesign and staff workflow updates
Scope
Order details, production activities, scheduling, staff rates
Platform
Web & mobile
01

Context

How can an order view serve the people taking the order and the people making it?

A tailoring order contains more than a purchase record. It also carries production activities, dates, measurements, and instructions. The order view has to give staff a way to find those details as the work moves forward.

Explore the workflow

Three views into the design work.

01 / ORDER VIEW

Organize the record around the job

I revised the order view to give manufacturing activities, scheduled dates, voice instructions, and additional details a place in the same workspace.

Design lens · Information hierarchy
02 / PRODUCTION

Give production details their own space

The design separates manufacturing activities from scheduled dates so staff can look up the work and its timing independently. Voice instructions sit alongside other order details.

Design lens · Operational handoffs
03 / STAFF RATES

Make per-work rates easier to reference

I also updated the per-work staff view, rate chart, and rate chart history. The history is a separate reference for earlier rates.

Design lens · Reference and history
