HR MANAGEMENT

Making attendance easier to review.

An HR platform for employees and administrators. I designed views for attendance records, correction requests, and the policies behind them.

Role
Attendance, employee portal, and HR settings design
Scope
Attendance, correction requests, policy settings, employee records
Platform
Web app
01

Context

How can employees and administrators work from the same attendance record?

An attendance record has several audiences. Employees may need to review a record or request a correction. Administrators need to see exceptions and configure the rules that apply to them.

Explore the workflow

Three views into the design work.

01 / ATTENDANCE

Create a useful starting point

I worked on the attendance record and its present, absent, on leave, and late views. Each status gives an administrator a different starting point for review.

Design lens · Status and navigation
02 / CORRECTIONS

Support both sides of a correction

I designed employee portal and administrator views for attendance regularization, including how to add and review a correction request.

Design lens · Role-based workflows
03 / POLICIES

Give policy settings a clear home

I designed attendance rules, break and deduction settings, and regularization settings, keeping configuration distinct from the daily record.

Design lens · Configuration and records
